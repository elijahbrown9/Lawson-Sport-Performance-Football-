Athlete Ratings · All Measured Metrics

The Standard

Every score is a percentile against this roster — a 90 in Strength means the athlete out-lifts 90% of the team. Every athlete is ranked. Categories not yet tested carry a conservative estimate — marked EST with a hatched bar — and are replaced by real numbers as soon as the test is in.

Elite 80–100 overall Advanced 65–79 Solid 45–64 Developing under 45 ▼ category to develop next EST estimated — not yet tested

How to read these ratings

Six categories. One standard.

Explosiveness (vertical + broad jump) · Upper Body (bench press) · Lower Body (front squat + deadlifts) · Rel. Strength (max pull-ups) · Speed & Agility (5-10-5) · Engine (mile). Balance measures how well-rounded an athlete is — 100 means every category is at the same level; the ▼ marks the category to develop next. Untested categories count at a conservative EST value, so completing the full battery is the fastest way to move up.
