How to read it

Compare each player to himself, not to teammates. Speed is red at −0.5 MPH vs a player's own prior average — nobody hits it: four games in, every skill player is within 0.5 MPH of where he started. ROLE-DRIVEN marks linemen — their peak swings with special-teams reps, so read a lineman's snap count, not his speed. Tested MPH is each player's Fly 10 gate from the Sept 1 retest.
